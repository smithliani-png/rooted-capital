Investing in theRoots of Tomorrow

Rooted Capital was founded on a simple belief: the strongest communities are the ones that can feed themselves. What began as a small family trust supporting local market gardens has grown into a fund dedicated to backing bold, practical ideas in sustainable food security.

Who We Are

We look for young, sharp-thinking teams who are not afraid of hard numbers and harder questions. When we find them, we invest early, and we invest generously.
